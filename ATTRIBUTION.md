# Attribution & Source Licenses

This corpus aggregates documents from several sources. Ground-truth annotations,
schemas, and evaluation tooling are the corpus authors' own work. Source documents
are credited below; per-document provenance is in each `*/manifests/*.json`
(`source_name`, `source_url`, `license`, `attribution`).

## Required attribution

### SROIE — ICDAR 2019 Robust Reading Challenge on Scanned Receipts (receipts)
- **Source:** ICDAR 2019 Robust Reading Challenge on SROIE — https://rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=13
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Changes:** documents were parsed to markdown and re-annotated with field-level ground truth for this corpus.

### MTSamples — Sample Medical Transcriptions (medical_records)
- **Source:** www.mtsamples.com
- **Terms:** used and shared for educational purposes with attribution, per the mtsamples.com terms of use ("You may print, share, or link to these sample reports for educational purposes"). Names and dates were changed/removed by the source for confidentiality.
- **Changes:** documents were parsed to markdown and re-annotated with field-level ground truth for this corpus.

## Public-domain sources (attribution courtesy, not required)

- **SEC EDGAR** (sec_filings, contracts) — mandatory public disclosures filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission; https://www.sec.gov/edgar. Copyright is nominally retained by the filing registrant; redistribution is standard for mandatory public filings.
- **CourtListener** (legal_filings) — U.S. court opinions, public domain (no copyright in U.S. government works); https://www.courtlistener.com.
- **Caselaw Access Project**, Harvard Law School (legal_filings) — CC0 / public domain; https://case.law.
- **U.S. federal & state government forms** (insurance_claims) — public-domain government works (DOL, FEMA, state workers-comp agencies).

## Synthetic documents

Synthetic documents (`source: synthetic` in the manifests) are generated by the
corpus authors and released under CC0 / no restriction.

## Excluded

ACORD and ISO insurance form layouts are copyrighted and are **not** redistributed
in this corpus; the corresponding categories use synthetic, functionally-equivalent
documents that do not reproduce ACORD/ISO layouts or text.
