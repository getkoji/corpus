# Data licensing

`LICENSE` (Apache-2.0) covers **code only** — `scripts/`, schemas, and tooling.

**It does not and cannot cover the documents.** This corpus aggregates material from
multiple third-party sources with different terms. Each document's terms are recorded in
its manifest (`<category>/manifests/<doc>.json` → `license`, `license_url`,
`attribution`, `license_basis`). This file is the summary.

**Last audited:** 2026-07-22. Remediation is **in progress** — see [Open issues](#open-issues).

## Our own work

| Artifact | License |
|---|---|
| **Ground-truth annotations** (`*/expected/*.json`, `truth.json`) | CC0 1.0 |
| **Synthetic documents** (generated by `scripts/`) | CC0 1.0 |
| **Schemas** (`*/schemas/*.yaml`) | Apache-2.0 |
| **Code** (`scripts/`) | Apache-2.0 |

> Synthetic documents and annotations were previously labelled Apache-2.0 in their
> manifests. Apache-2.0 is a *software* licence and is ambiguous when applied to data;
> CC0 is the intended grant. Manifest relabelling is pending — see Open issues.

### On EDGAR (the largest block)

A 10-K or 8-K exhibit is authored by the registrant, not the SEC. Per the US interagency
[CENDI copyright FAQ](https://cendi.gov/pdf/FAQ_Copyright_30jan18.pdf), *"the fact that a
privately created work is, with permission, included in a U.S. Government work does not
place the private work into the public domain."*

These documents were previously mislabelled `Public Domain (US Government Work)` and
attributed to the SEC. They are now labelled with no-explicit-licence and attributed to
the filing registrant. Redistribution here rests on their status as mandatory public
disclosure records used for non-commercial research — a reasoned position, not a licence
grant.

### On ISO form *codes* vs form *text*

Synthetic documents in this corpus reference ISO form numbers (`CG 00 01`, `CG 20 26`,
`CA 20 48`). A form number is a fact, not protected expression, and its use is fine.
Reproducing the full ISO form **text** is a different matter and is covered by the
🔴 row above.

## Open issues

Findings from the 2026-07-22 audit that are **not yet remediated**. Do not treat this
corpus as licence-clean until they are closed.

1. **MTSamples (100 docs)** — the manifests claim CC0. That label originates from a
   third-party Kaggle upload, not from MTSamples, and there is no chain of title.
   Planned: ship a downloader + our annotations instead of the documents.
2. **ACORD certificates (~18 docs)** — manifests say "Public record — state government
   website", which describes where a document was found, not a licence. Planned: remove
   and substitute existing synthetic COIs.
3. **ISO / insurer specimen policies (~5 docs)** — same defect. Planned: remove.
4. **SROIE attribution (52 docs)** — CC BY 4.0 requires attribution the manifests do not
   carry. Planned: add attribution; verify terms against the official RRC source.
5. **Synthetic + annotations still labelled Apache-2.0 in manifests (431 docs)** —
   should be CC0.

## Reporting a licensing problem

Open an issue, or contact the maintainers. If you hold rights in material here and want
it removed, say so and it will be removed promptly — no argument.
